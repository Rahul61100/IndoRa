# HDFC Bank's margin problem is post-merger structure, not the rate cycle

Q1 FY27 NIM printed **3.26%**, a record low, down 12 bps sequentially. CASA ratio 32%. Historic
blended NIM was above 4%.

Cause: the HDFC Ltd merger brought a large low-yielding mortgage book (2-2.5% NIM) funded by
high-cost borrowings. Not a rate-cycle effect.

The recovery levers are mechanical rather than hopeful, which is the actual bull case:
- Borrowings down to **11% of liabilities from 21% in Sep 2023**, heading toward the 5-6%
  industry norm as **₹40,000-50,000 crore matures over roughly two years**
- Time-deposit repricing runs on a **~5-quarter lag**; management says about **two-thirds** is
  through
- Retail funding mix targeted at 60% from 52%

But management themselves called it a multi-quarter journey, and analysts trimmed FY27-28 NII
estimates 2-4% after the Q1 print.

Price context 2026-08-26: ₹727.2, -26.0% over a year, 14.3% below its 200 DMA, RSI 38, **1.0%
above its 52-week low**, relative strength -24.7% vs Nifty. FPIs have cut their holding five
consecutive quarters. Roughly ₹4 lakh crore of market cap gone in 2026.

**How to apply:** this is a multi-quarter accumulation, not a bounce trade. There is no technical
base to stop against. The first honest test is the Q2 FY27 NIM print in October.
