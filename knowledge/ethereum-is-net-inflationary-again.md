# Ethereum has flipped back to net inflationary — the L2s took the fees

Verified 2026-08-26 from primary sources (ultrasound.money burn API, validatorqueue.com).

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| ETH staked | **42.4M (34.78% of supply)**, 903,265 active validators |
| Staking APR | 2.7% |
| Gross issuance | ~1.145M ETH/yr (~3,137 ETH/day) |
| **Burn, 30-day average** | **~45 ETH/day** — L1 fee revenue of roughly **$68,000 per day** |
| **Net issuance** | **≈ +3,092 ETH/day ≈ +0.9% per year — INFLATIONARY** |
| Cumulative burn since Merge | 2,014,613 ETH (~$4.55bn) |
| Validator entry queue | 2,192,418 ETH, **~38 days** wait (exit queue: 18 minutes) |

*(The net-issuance figure is derived from the two sourced inputs above, not a single reported
number — directional, not exact.)*

**The "ultrasound money" deflationary thesis is not currently operative**, and the reason is
structural rather than cyclical: **the L2s took the fee revenue and then competed it to zero.**

That closes the loop with [[l2s-are-consolidating-not-growing]] — Dencun cut L2 fees ~90%,
triggering a fee war in which only Base turned a profit in 2025 (~$55mn). The activity moved off
L1, the L1 burn collapsed with it, and **neither layer captured the value.** Ethereum scaled
successfully and destroyed its own economics doing it.

**Note the queue asymmetry as a sentiment read:** 38 days to enter staking against 18 minutes to
exit. Capital still wants in at a 2.7% yield. That is genuine demand for the asset — it just is
not showing up in the token's monetary economics.

**How to apply:** stop citing ETH's deflationary supply as a bull point; it is factually not the
case at present. The ETH investment case now has to rest on staking yield, ETF flows, or L2
sequencer economics accruing back to L1 — and the third has demonstrably failed so far. This is
also the cleanest case study available anywhere of **successful technology adoption with zero
value accrual to the token**, and it should be the reference example the next time any
infrastructure thesis in any market equates usage with returns.
