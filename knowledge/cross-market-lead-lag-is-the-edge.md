# The reason to run multiple markets is lead-lag, not diversification

Design principle for this workspace, adopted 2026-08-26 when the first US pull immediately
explained something the India-only view could not.

Running India, US and crypto side by side is **not** about spreading money across geographies.
It is about the fact that the same global narrative arrives in different markets at different
times, and the market it hits first is a free preview of the market it hits second.

Confirmed instances so far:
- **AI-disrupts-software** broke US SaaS in Feb 2026 and had reversed there by April. Indian IT
  is still discounting it in August. [[us-saas-already-bottomed-india-it-has-not]]
- **The rate cycle turned globally, not locally.** RBI has held four meetings; the Fed has held
  five with three dissents wanting a *hike*. Neither is easing. Any India thesis resting on cuts
  is fighting a global regime, not a domestic one. [[rate-cut-cycle-is-over]], [[fed-may-hike-next]]
- **Small-cap leadership inverted between the two markets.** Russell 2000 +28.7% over a year
  against S&P +19.2%, driven by OBBBA tax provisions and 17.1% expected small-cap earnings growth.
  Indian small caps are also outperforming but on a **34.4x P/E against a 28.3x median** with no
  equivalent earnings catalyst. Same price behaviour, opposite fundamental backing.

**How to apply:** every daily loop should ask one explicit question — *what is happening in one
market that has not yet been priced in another?* Log candidate lead-lags here even when the
trade is not taken; the record of whether the lag actually resolved is the only way to learn
which linkages are real. Beware the failure mode: a correlation of narratives is not a
correlation of earnings, and the read-across breaks wherever the business models differ.
