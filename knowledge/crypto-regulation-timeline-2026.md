# US crypto regulation: the laws passed in 2025, the rules land late 2026 into 2027

Verified 2026-08-26. Useful because the *gap* between statute and rulemaking is where the
mispricing lives.

**GENIUS Act** — passed July 2025, the stablecoin framework. Licensing, capital, custody and AML
rules carry **key 2026 deadlines**. It **bans stablecoin issuers from paying yield** — but it
explicitly excludes DeFi protocols, distributed-ledger protocols and liquidity-pool participation
from the definition of a regulated entity. So users can still earn a return by deploying
stablecoins *into* DeFi; the issuer just cannot pay it.

**CLARITY Act** — the market-structure bill; passed the House in July 2025. Gives the **CFTC
exclusive jurisdiction over spot markets for digital commodities**, including BTC, ETH and most
non-security blockchain-native tokens. The SEC retains primary-market fundraising for tokens that
begin as securities. It also draws the first regulatory boundary around DeFi and RWA tokenisation.

**Timing is the point:** SEC and CFTC rulemaking could take up to **18 months**, with the main
rules likely effective **late 2026 or 2027**.

**Why it matters:** the structural bull case for tokenisation and regulated DeFi is real but the
enabling rules are not in force yet. Anything priced today on "regulatory clarity" is priced on
an expectation with a multi-quarter lag and real implementation risk.

**How to apply:** watch the rulemaking calendar rather than the headlines. The
yield-ban-with-a-DeFi-carve-out is the specific asymmetry worth tracking — it channels demand for
stablecoin yield toward protocols rather than issuers, which is a durable flow, not a trade.
Note the second-order equity read: Circle (CRCL), Coinbase (COIN) and the exchange complex sit
directly on this boundary. COIN is -40% over a year. **No position taken.**
