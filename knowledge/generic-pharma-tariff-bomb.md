# US generics face 100% tariffs in 2028 and 200% in 2029 — CDMO does not

Under the India-US trade deal announced 2 Feb 2026, headline tariffs on Indian goods fell from
50% to **18%**, the punitive 25% removed entirely. India reciprocated with movement toward zero
tariffs, halting Russian crude purchases and committing to US energy and technology purchases.

The exception is generic pharmaceuticals, on a deliberate escalation schedule:
- **Zero tariff for two years from 1 August 2026**
- **100% from August 2028**
- **200% from August 2029**

Stated purpose is to force reshoring of generic manufacturing into the US. It applies to generics
only; patented and branded medicines are unchanged.

**Why it matters:** this is a dated, legislated impairment of the US-generics business model, and
it sits inside the holding period of any medium or long horizon position. It does **not** touch
CDMO, API-for-innovator or domestic-formulation businesses.

**How to apply:** for pharma exposure past 2028, prefer CDMO and domestic formulations over US
generics. Divi's Laboratories is the live example of the market already voting this way:
+49.7% over a year, at its 52-week high on 2026-08-26, Q1 FY27 PAT +65.5% to ₹902 crore on
revenue +24.3%, riding custom-synthesis order conversion plus China+1 and BIOSECURE.
Sun Pharma, Dr Reddy's and Cipla carry the US-generics exposure.
