# The yen carry trade was squeezed in August and has already re-loaded

Verified 2026-08-26. **Assessed as the highest-conviction hedgeable risk in the current map**, and
it was entirely absent from my analysis.

**What happened:**
- The yen fell to **~164/USD in late July — its weakest in roughly 40 years**
- That triggered the **first coordinated US-Japan yen-buying intervention since 1998**, on the
  first Friday of August, pushing the yen to ~155
- It has since drifted back to **159-160**, giving back most of the bounce

**Why the setup is now worse, not better:**
- **BOJ policy rate is 1.0%** as of July 2026 — the highest since September 1995, after a 25bp
  June hike. Markets price **1.25% by quarter-end**; board member Takata already dissented for an
  immediate move. So the rate differential is mechanically narrowing.
- Hedge funds hold **near-record short-yen positioning — roughly 124,575 contracts, about $9.5bn,
  close to the largest since 2007**
- And the intervention **handed carry traders a better re-entry**: Japanese investors net bought
  **over ¥5 trillion of foreign equities and bonds in the two weeks to 15 August** — money
  recycling straight back into the trade the BOJ was trying to unwind

**The leverage was not flushed. It was re-loaded at a better price.**

**Why it matters more than its headline suggests:** a carry unwind is **mechanical, not
narrative**. Yen appreciation forces simultaneous selling across everything the carry funded —
historically EM FX, US high-beta tech, and crypto — producing a correlated drawdown across
otherwise unrelated assets, fast. The August 2024 episode is the precedent, and **all its
ingredients are present again, arguably more so.**

**Most exposed: crypto.** In the 2024 precedent it was among the hardest and fastest hit relative
to its own volatility — a favoured high-beta target for carry-funded leverage with no independent
fundamental anchor to arrest the move. That compounds with the finding that the current crypto
rally is imported liquidity ([[stablecoin-supply-peaked-in-may]]) and that crypto runs 0.3-0.5
correlated with US equities ([[cross-market-correlation-needs-a-lag]]).

**How to apply:** **add USDJPY, the BOJ policy rate and CFTC yen positioning to the daily macro
pull** — none are currently tracked. Watch the 160 line as the intervention trigger. This is the
one risk in the map that hits a book with no warning and no narrative, and it is the reason a
correlation matrix computed in calm conditions understates true drawdown correlation.
