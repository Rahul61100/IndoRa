# The monsoon deficit stopped moving, and the headline stopped tracking the ground

Two days of collection undercut the framing I put on this trade, without changing its direction.

## The number is flat, not closing

The national rainfall deficit has **sat in the 13–15% band since late July** — not narrowing, not
widening. So the dated, falsifiable setup recorded on 27 August ("a deficit that narrows through
September flips this trade hard") has simply **not triggered either way.** That is the honest status.

## But the headline and the ground have come apart

| indicator | reading |
|---|---|
| national deficit | −13 to −15%, flat |
| deficient districts | **69 → 412** *(base figure unverified)* |
| kharif sowing | **96% of normal** — recovered |
| reservoir levels | **60%+ nationally** — moderate |

Deficiency spread across far more districts while **sowing recovered and reservoirs held.** Those
should move together and did not. Either the district count is measuring something narrower than
crop stress, or irrigation and storage absorbed the shortfall — and I cannot yet say which.

Regional concentration is severe and is probably the real story:

**Andhra Pradesh −51% · Meghalaya −58% · Punjab −34% · Karnataka −31%**

## What I got wrong

I framed this as a **national binary**: deficit closes, rural trade flips. The evidence supports a
**regional and crop-specific** read instead. A national aggregate that moves independently of
sowing and storage is exactly the kind of number this base has already learned not to trust —
[[india-aggregate-earnings-are-three-companies]] is the same lesson in a different series.

Also worth flagging: **"weakest since 2009" remains an open question** in the two outlets that
raised it, not a settled fact, and I had been repeating it as settled.

## What does not change

The spread monitor still reads **urban over rural, widening.** The trade is working; my explanation
for it was too coarse. Do not add rural-exposed consumption on cheapness — but stop expecting one
national print to flip it.

**Source:** agent research against secondary outlets, 2026-08-29. The 13–15% band, the district
counts and the sowing figure are all **reported**, not verified against an IMD primary release.
The 69→412 district jump rests on an unverified base and is the single number here most worth
checking directly.
