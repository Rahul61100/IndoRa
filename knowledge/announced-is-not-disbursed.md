# Announced is not disbursed — and in India the gap is most of the story

Verified 2026-08-26. **Confidence: DEGRADED** — this agent ran after the session search cap, via
Bing News aggregation with many primary outlets blocking direct fetch. Directionally strong,
individually re-verify before acting. See [`research-budget-constraints`](../playbooks/research-budget-constraints.md).

**The single most useful distinction in Indian policy investing, and I had not been making it.**
Every PLI and mission number I quoted previously was an *announcement*. Here is what has actually
been paid.

## PLI: ₹35,354 crore disbursed against >₹2.4 lakh crore of "investment attracted"

| Sector | Actually disbursed | Announced / committed |
|---|---|---|
| **Electronics** | **₹15,554 cr** | best-disbursed sector by far |
| Food processing | ₹1,155 cr (to Feb 2025) | ₹9,207 cr investment attracted |
| Telecom equipment | ₹1,597.93 cr (FY25-26) | >₹5,200 cr attracted |
| **Auto and auto components** | **₹2,321 cr of a ₹25,938 cr outlay — under 10%** | Budget 2026 **doubled** the FY26-27 allocation to ₹5,940 cr *despite* the low uptake |
| **Pharma / bulk drugs API** | **1.3% disbursed** | capacity built for 28 APIs; 10 projects not yet at commercial production |
| ACC battery (Ola Cell) | nothing yet | up to ₹7,240 cr approved; quarterly disbursement begins ~Aug 2026 |
| Specialty steel | none confirmed | ₹11,887 cr of MoUs across 55 companies |
| White goods (round 4) | none confirmed | 5 companies, ₹863 cr committed |
| Solar PV | none | MNRE *weighing* ₹7,000-8,000 cr VGF for polysilicon |
| Drones | Drone PLI 2.0 **not yet notified** | ideaForge's ₹151 cr came under RDI, a different scheme |

## The sharpest example: green hydrogen

**₹292 crore disbursed** against **756,100 tonnes/year of production capacity and 3,000 MW/year of
electrolyser capacity already "awarded".** The FY26-27 Budget kept the Mission's allocation **flat
at ₹600 crore.**

That is the announced-versus-real distinction in one line.

## Semicon 2.0 — a genuine four-way spread in maturity

The ₹1.27 lakh crore headline hides very different realities:

| Project | Status |
|---|---|
| **Micron, Sanand ATMP** | **Operational** — inaugurated 28 Feb 2026, $2.75bn |
| **CG Power (CG Semi), Sanand OSAT** | **Commercial production live** — launched 4 Jul 2026, ₹7,600 cr |
| **Kaynes Semicon, Sanand OSAT** | Inaugurated 31 Mar 2026, ramping; mass production targeted Sep 2026 |
| **Tata Electronics, Dholera 300mm fab** | **Slipped** — commercial production pushed to **mid-2028**, and the **node dropped from 28nm to 90nm** |
| **HCL-Foxconn, Jewar OSAT** | **Foundation stone only**, ops targeted 2027-28 — roughly 18-24 months behind Sanand |

**Mobile PLI 2.0 (MPMS) was only cleared on 15 July 2026 at ₹62,500 crore. No disbursement is
possible yet.** The names that rallied on clearance — Dixon, Kaynes, Cyient DLM, Syrma, Amber —
rallied on an approval, not a payment.

## Defence: an Acceptance of Necessity is not a contract

The **~₹52,000 crore DAC approval on 3 July 2026** is a *go-ahead to procure*, not signed orders.
Actual signed 2025-26 contracts are an order of magnitude smaller: Bharat Dynamics ₹1,347.71 cr,
BEL ₹1,251 cr then ₹572 cr then ₹541 cr, Data Patterns ₹585.76 cr, Bharat Forge ₹425 cr, Zen ₹295
cr, BEML ₹184 cr. Data Patterns' ₹1,300 cr HAL order is **L1 only — not yet signed.**

## And two contrarian findings that cut against the whole "government largesse" narrative

- **NHAI road-project awards are at a seven-year low**, NHAI debt is down 43% from its FY22 peak,
  and awards are "unlikely to rebound in FY27 amid flat capex". Highway EPC momentum is
  **declining**, not accelerating. PNC Infratech received an NHAI show-cause notice in August.
- **No NIP or Gati-Shakti-labelled awards were found at all.**

## How to apply

**Always ask: approved, awarded, or paid?** Three different things, routinely reported as one.
A scheme with 1.3% disbursement is a press release. Track the **disbursement line in budget
documents**, not the headline outlay. Related: [[india-capex-is-central-government-and-front-loaded]].
