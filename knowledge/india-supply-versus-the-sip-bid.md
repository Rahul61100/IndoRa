# India's domestic bid is enormous, and so is the supply arriving to meet it

Verified 2026-08-26. Both sides are structural now, which is why the index goes nowhere while
earnings grow ([[india-is-derating-not-missing-earnings]]).

**The bid — domestic, mechanical, and record-setting:**
- Monthly **SIP inflows near ₹31,000 crore** (₹31,115 crore in April 2026, +16.8% year on year;
  ~₹31,000 crore in June against ₹28,464 crore a year earlier)
- **DII net buying streak of 25 consecutive months, ₹11.4 trillion cumulative**
- **DII ownership of Nifty 500 companies at a record 21%** as of June 2026
- Over the 22 months since the September 2024 peak, cumulative DII inflows have comfortably
  absorbed sustained FII outflows
- FPIs turned net buyers in July 2026 (₹20,200 crore) after four months of selling, and added
  ₹23,544 crore in August

**The supply — also record-setting:**
- **245 DRHPs filed, 175 under SEBI observation; 238 companies seeking ₹4.72 trillion**
- Names in the queue: **Jio Platforms, NSE, PhonePe, SBI Funds Management, Zepto, Avaada Electro,
  Oravel Stays (OYO)**
- A further ~$6bn of secondary equity offerings lined up for H2 2026
- Jefferies separately flags $50-70bn of total equity supply capping how far the market can
  re-rate even if earnings deliver
- Mitigating: much of the released stock sits with promoters and promoter-group entities and
  would not all translate into selling

**Why it matters:** the SIP bid is why India has not fallen despite the de-rating, and the supply
pipeline is why it has not risen despite 18% profit growth. Neither is a sentiment variable — both
are mechanical and both are measurable, which makes their *ratio* the single best framing for the
index.

**How to apply:** track monthly SIP and DII net against the IPO/OFS calendar. When a ₹50,000
crore-plus issue prices — Jio and NSE are the two that matter — it drains the same pool the SIP
bid fills, and that is an index-level event, not a stock-level one. **Neither series is collected
by the pipeline yet; this is the highest-value Phase 1 gap.** Related: [`roadmap`](../playbooks/roadmap.md).
