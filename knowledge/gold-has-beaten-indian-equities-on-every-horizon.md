# Gold has beaten the Nifty over 1, 3 AND 5 years — in rupees

**This is the answer to the question I froze the India book on**: at a negative equity risk premium
([[CORRECTION-india-erp-is-negative]]), why own Indian equities? Here is what the alternative
actually delivered. Computed from a consistent series (COMEX gold × same-day USDINR, scaled to
10g) so all four dates are on the same basis.

| Horizon | **Gold (INR)** | **Nifty 50** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | **+45.9%** | **−0.9%** |
| 3 year CAGR | **+40.4%** | **+7.9%** |
| 5 year CAGR | **+27.2%** | **+7.2%** |

Levels: gold ₹1,42,743/10g today against ₹97,823 a year ago, ₹51,535 in Aug 2023 and ₹42,836 in
Aug 2021. Nifty 24,207.75 against 24,426.85 a year ago.

**Gold has not merely beaten Indian equities — it has beaten them on every horizon measured, by
wide margins, for five years.** This is a sustained re-rating, not a one-year blip.

**Basis note that matters:** the retail/MCX quote is ~₹1,63,750/10g, about 14-15% *above* the
futures-equivalent series above, because retail carries import duty, GST and dealer premium. Use
the retail number for "what would I pay", the series above for "what was the return". Do not mix
them.

**Two things complicate the obvious conclusion:**

1. **The cheapest vehicle is closing.** Sovereign Gold Bonds remain discontinued for new issuance —
   RBI's only 2026 item is a premature-redemption calendar for Oct 2026-Mar 2027. And **Union
   Budget 2026 restricted the capital-gains exemption on SGB redemption to original allottees only,
   effective April 2026.** That is expected to compress or eliminate the ~15% secondary-market
   premium SGBs historically traded at, and it closes off the zero-capital-gains-at-maturity route
   for new buyers. ETFs and physical remain, both taxed.

2. **The marginal Indian buyer is not chasing it.** Gold ETF net flows: **January +₹24,040 crore
   (a record)**, then February +₹5,254, March +₹2,265 (−57%), April +₹3,040, **May −₹725 — the
   first outflow in thirteen months**, June +₹3,443, July +₹1,559 (−55%). Decelerating through the
   year despite the price. Retail conviction is fading even as the return is spectacular.

**How to apply:** whenever an Indian equity thesis is written, the honest comparison set is not
"Nifty versus its own history". It is **Nifty versus a 6.85% G-sec versus gold**. On a five-year
look-back gold won, and on the current ERP the G-sec wins prospectively. That does not mean sell
equities — past gold returns are not a forecast, and the flow data says the easy money there may
be behind us. It means **the burden of proof on any India equity position is much higher than I
had been applying.** Related: [[india-in-usd-is-the-return-that-matters]], [[macro-regime-aug-2026]].
