# Macro regime as of August 2026 — oil deflating, rupee weak, gold in a rebound not a breakout

Levels from the 2026-08-26 snapshot.

- **Brent $86.5**, +25.6% over a year but **-26.9% from its 52-week high** and -8.3% over three
  months. EIA sees Q3 2026 averaging ~$85 and **2027 averaging $69**, with ~0.6 mb/d of Hormuz
  disruption persisting. The oil spike is deflating, not building.
- **USDINR 95.4** — the rupee is 8.3% weaker over a year; FY26 alone was -9.88%, the steepest in
  fourteen years. A margin tailwind for exporters, an inflation and import-bill problem otherwise.
  India has also stopped buying discounted Russian crude under the trade deal.
- **Gold $4,679.9**, +38.7% over a year and +15.9% in a month, RSI 76 — but **-12.0% from its
  52-week high.** Gold made an all-time high of $5,589 on 28 Jan 2026, then fell over 20% in its
  worst quarter since 2013. What looks like a breakout is a rebound inside a correction.
  **Silver $68.3, +76.6% over a year** but 40.7% below its high, same shape.
- **US 10-year 4.7%**, rising. Dollar index 99.2, drifting.
- **India VIX 10.6**, 52-week range 8.72 to 28.90 — near the low end. Equity weakness has not
  produced any volatility repricing. Hedges are cheap; tail risk is underpriced.
- **Monsoon:** cumulative rainfall 11.5% below LPA at 1 August, El Niño strengthening, IMD
  forecasting below-normal August and September. 397 of 741 districts below normal. Kharif sowing
  -3% year on year at end-July after -23% at end-June. Direct pressure on rural income and food
  inflation — and the likely cause of FMCG's -20.9% year.

**How to apply:** an oil-beneficiary thesis (ONGC, Oil India) is fighting both the forward curve
and 0% sector breadth. A weak-rupee beneficiary thesis (IT, pharma exporters) has a real tailwind
but must clear the AI and tariff questions separately. Do not describe gold as breaking out.
