# The capex question splits on lagging versus forward data, and averaging them destroys the signal

The capex-over-consumption spread has been **widening for six months** (+19.8pp, +8.0pp in the last
month). This base flagged that it could not tell whether that was **a real cycle or a market
rotation**, and said the activity data would decide it.

The activity data arrived and split — cleanly, along a line worth naming.

## For a real cycle

- **IIP use-based breakdown, July**: *"capital goods surge as consumer non-durables contract."*
  Exactly the production-side evidence that would validate the thesis.
- **Record Q2 peak power demand: 270.8 GW, +12% YoY.**

## Against

- **The power record is attributed to heatwave AC load, not industrial load.** It is a weather
  print wearing an industrial costume, and it is weaker evidence than the headline suggests.
- **Manufacturing PMI flash 52.9 (August)** — the weakest improvement in factory conditions
  **since August 2021**, new orders and output at a five-year-slow pace, and manufacturing
  **employment contracting for the first time in 2.5 years.**
- **IIP itself decelerated**: +6.7% YoY in July against a revised +8.8% in June.
- **E-way bill volume growth +6%** in July against +14.5% in June, while GST *value* growth
  accelerated to +15.4% — a volume-versus-value divergence, and the GST print is confounded by
  GST 2.0 base effects rolling through the YoY window.

## The distinction that matters, and the rule that follows

**Do not average these.** They are not two noisy measurements of one quantity.

**IIP is a lagging production index. PMI is a forward-looking survey.** IIP says investment goods
*already produced* outran consumption goods. PMI says *incoming* orders and momentum are
decelerating and firms are shedding manufacturing workers. Both can be true simultaneously, and
they describe different points in the chain: what was made last month versus what is being ordered
now.

Averaging them into "mixed signals" throws away the only information the pair contains — **the
sequence.** A lagging index strong while a forward survey weakens is a specific, recognisable
pattern, and it is not the pattern of a cycle beginning.

This is the same error class as [[india-aggregate-earnings-are-three-companies]] and
[[the-monsoon-headline-and-the-ground-have-decoupled]]: two series that should move together are
not, and the temptation each time is to blend them into a single comfortable number rather than ask
why they diverge.

## Verdict

**Held open, deliberately.** The spread keeps working, so the trade is fine; the *explanation* is
not established. The single number that would settle it is the **actual IIP capital-goods
percentage**, which is currently headline-only — the figure was never retrieved, only the phrase.
That is the next thing to chase.

What I will not do is let a widening spread stand as its own evidence. A market rotation and a
capital cycle look identical in price for a long time, and only one of them survives a PMI at a
five-year low.
