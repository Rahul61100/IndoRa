# Indian IT de-rated on AI disruption, and the guidance is still being cut

The four largest Indian IT names are down at least 50% from their all-time highs; roughly
₹19.28 lakh crore of market value has gone across ten IT companies. The proximate trigger was
Accenture cutting its annual revenue forecast on 19 June 2026 and falling ~18% in a session, its
worst ever. The structural fear is Anthropic and OpenAI moving from models into enterprise
services.

The critical detail, and the reason this is not simply a cheapness opportunity: **Infosys trimmed
its FY27 revenue guidance to 1.5-3.0% at the Q1 FY27 print.** Revisions are still going the wrong
way.

Q1 FY27 Infosys specifics: revenue $5.08bn (+2.4% constant currency), operating margin 21.1%,
large-deal TCV $3.6bn with 61% net new, free cash flow $955mn, **AI already 8.2% of revenue**.
TCS FY26 TCV was $40.7bn.

Prices 2026-08-26: Infosys ₹1,120 (-24.7% 1y, -33.7% from high, 15.2% below 200 DMA),
TCS ₹2,270 (-25.7% 1y), Wipro ₹177.3 (-28.7%). The constituent basket is -11.4% over a year but
**+5.9% over three months** with 43% of members above their 200 DMA — an attempted base, not a
confirmed turn.

**How to apply:** the valuation and the ~4% dividend yield are real, but negative revisions
disqualify this as a short-horizon idea. Medium horizon at most, entered in tranches. The signal
to watch is a guidance *raise*, not a cheap multiple.
