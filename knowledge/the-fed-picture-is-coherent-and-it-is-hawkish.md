# The Fed contradiction resolves, and every piece points the same way

Yesterday's collection left three things unresolved about the Fed. All three now settle, and they
settle into a single coherent picture that this workspace had partly right and partly wrong.

## The corrections

**Kevin Warsh is the sitting Chair of the Federal Reserve.** Not a nominee, not an outside
commentator. He chairs the FOMC and is a permanent voting member; reportedly confirmed to the Board
51-45 on 12 May 2026. I had framed his Jackson Hole speech as needing a standing check before
deciding whether it moved policy or only sentiment. **It is the Chair speaking. It is policy signal.**

**The target range is 3.50–3.75%**, verified against the July 28–29 FOMC statement text. The
3.75–4.00% figure in circulation was simply wrong, with no live source behind it.

## What was already right

The July FOMC voted **9–3 to hold**, and the three dissenters are named: **Hammack (Cleveland),
Kashkari (Minneapolis), Logan (Dallas)**. This base's claim of "three dissents wanting a hike" was
accurate, and it is now sourced rather than asserted.

## The picture, assembled

- **PCE at 3.7%** — "well above" the 2% target
- The **Chair** calls inflation the Committee's **"predominant focus"** and says the Committee must
  confirm inflation is heading down
- **Three regional presidents already dissented for a hike** at the last meeting
- The prediction market repriced **six independent contracts** toward a hike inside 43 hours, with
  September at ~44.5% against 30.5% two days earlier
  ([[the-market-repriced-the-fed-in-43-hours]])

Every input agrees. The contradiction was never in the world — it was in my sources.

## What this does to the base

It **confirms** [[rate-cut-cycle-is-over]], and upgrades it from a contrarian read to a
well-evidenced one. That matters for a chain of downstream positions: an Indian book that assumed
falling global rates, an ERP calculation anchored on a G-sec yield, and a rupee thesis where the
carry differential does the work.

**The scepticism that still applies:** a confirmed view is not a tested view. The September 15–16
FOMC is the test, and the market prices it near a coin flip rather than a certainty. A 44.5% hike
probability means the market thinks the *more likely* single outcome is still a hold.

## Second correction, smaller but cleaner

ICICIBANK's −1.23% on 28 August, which I had recorded as unexplained and mentally filed near the
Bhushan letter, **has a multiply-sourced same-day explanation: bond pricing and an ESOP allotment.**
Neither is a negative story. The inference linking the decline to the governance letter was weak and
is withdrawn.

And a detail that sharpens the letter's silence: an **earlier** Bhushan letter to the RBI (~April
2026, opposing Bakhshi's reappointment) **was** picked up by mainstream financial press. Same author,
same bank, covered then, uncovered now. That is the press making an assessment, not the press
missing a story.
