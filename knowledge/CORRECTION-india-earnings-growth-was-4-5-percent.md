# CORRECTION: India's FY26 earnings grew 4.5%, not 18% — and 60% of Q1 came from five stocks

**This corrects the framing I built the entire India case on.** I repeatedly wrote "earnings +18%
while the index is -5%" and called it a de-rating rather than an earnings problem. The 18% is real
but it is a **single quarter's PAT**, and it does not mean what I used it to mean.

**What the full-year record actually shows:**

| Fiscal year | Growth expected at the start | Growth actually delivered | Miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY25 | ~15% | **3.4%** | ~11.6pp |
| FY26 | 12-15% | **4.5%** | ~7.5-10.5pp |

**Two consecutive years of delivering roughly a third of what was promised.** Nomura notes
earnings have come in below start-of-year consensus in **each of the past nine years, with the
miss widening over the last four.**

**And the 18% quarter is narrower than it looks: 60% of the incremental profit came from five
companies** — ONGC, Hindalco, Reliance, JSW Steel and Bharti Airtel. That is a commodity base
effect plus telecom tariff hikes, not broad earnings power.

**Forward estimates were being cut the whole time.** FY27 consensus EPS fell roughly **9% over
twelve months** (~₹1,357 to ~₹1,235). In April 2026, 66% of Nifty constituents were cut on FY27
EPS; in May, 62%. BofA cut FY27 growth from 14% pre-conflict to 11% to **8.5%**.

**The revision cycle turned up only in the last four to six weeks** — post-Q1 upgrade/downgrade
ratio of 1.5x, strongest in 22 quarters — but the actual estimate change was **+0.6%**. One good
quarter after five-plus quarters of cuts is a data point, not a tested reversal.

**Three further facts that reframe the index specifically:**
- **Corporate profit-to-GDP is at an all-time high of 5.2%**, and DSP Netra flags PAT margins as
  "close to a peak". Margin expansion is a spent tailwind; growth now has to come from volume.
- **The Nifty 50's share of Nifty 500 profit fell from 87% in FY18 to 51% in FY26.** Q4 FY26 PAT
  grew 4.5% — the **eighth consecutive quarter of single-digit growth**. The index basket is
  structurally the weakest part of the market even while India Inc broadly does fine.
- At an **unchanged multiple**, FY27 consensus EPS is worth only about **+4%** index upside.
  Reaching the 28,000-30,000 targets in circulation needs either a large EPS beat *or* a re-rating
  back to the 22-23x average, or both.

**Names among the biggest FY27 downgrades:** HDFC Bank, IndiGo, Tata Steel, Maruti, Dr Reddy's and
**Infosys** — two of which are in the open book.

**Method lesson:** I took a quarterly print, did not check its composition, and did not check
whether forward estimates were moving in the same direction. **Trailing beats and forward cuts
routinely coexist. Always check the revision direction before calling a de-rating.**
