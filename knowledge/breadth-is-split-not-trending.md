# Breadth is split almost exactly down the middle — this is a selection market

From the 2026-08-26 snapshot across ~90 tracked Indian stocks:

- **56%** above their 200 DMA
- **49%** above their 50 DMA
- trend split: **24 uptrend / 23 downtrend / 25 choppy**

**Why it matters:** an index view is close to worthless at this reading. Half the market is
working and half is not, and the two halves are separated by sector, not by size or valuation.
Money made here comes from sector and stock selection, not from getting the Nifty right.

**How to apply:** stop weighting index targets heavily in any note. Lead with the sector rotation
table. Re-check this reading weekly — if it moves past roughly 70% or below roughly 30% above
the 200 DMA, an index view becomes worth having again.
