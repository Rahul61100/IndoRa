# Shriram Finance: the MUFG capital is real, the re-rating case is not yet proven

Verified 2026-08-27 against **primary regulatory filings** (SEBI Reg. 52(4), 24 July 2026) —
higher confidence than most notes here. NBFC-landscape items are news-sourced and DEGRADED.

## The transaction, verified

**MUFG Bank took 20% via preferential allotment — 471.12 million shares at ₹840.93, total
₹39,618 crore.** Terms first reported 22 Dec 2025, shareholders approved 14 Jan 2026, capital
received 8 April 2026, of which **₹37,451 crore was deployed within Q1 FY27**.

My three carried figures, checked:

| I had | Verdict |
|---|---|
| ~$4.4bn | ✅ upper end — ₹39,618 cr, quoted $4.26-4.4bn depending on outlet and FX date |
| capital adequacy to 34% | ✅ **CRAR 20.40% (Mar-26) → 34.17% (Jun-26)** — and it **beat** the ~31% guided in December |
| net worth +66% | ✅ **₹65,244 cr → ₹108,297 cr, +65.98%** — but note it is a **single-quarter jump from the infusion**, not organic |
| cost of funds −50-75bps | ❌ **wrong.** Guidance was **50-100bps**, and it is **forward guidance, not an actual** — no cost-of-funds line appears in the Q1 filing to verify against |

## Two traps this work-up caught

**1. The Q1 profit jump is flattered, and next quarter will look worse mechanically.**
PAT was **₹3,444.56 crore, +59.79% year on year**, with net margin up to 25.68% from 18.68%. But
**the equity base roughly doubled mid-quarter while EPS was not diluted for a full quarter.**
Once the new net worth sits in the average base, **RoE will mechanically fall even if operations
are fine.** Do not read this quarter as operating improvement.

**2. The P/B is disputed, and the two readings give opposite answers.**
- **Screener.in shows 3.18x.**
- **Recomputed from the primary filing:** net worth ₹108,297.48 crore ÷ ~232.3 crore shares
  (implied by PAT ÷ EPS) = **BVPS ≈ ₹466**, so at ₹1,102 the multiple is **≈2.36x**.

The likely cause is a stale book-value denominator at the aggregator following the April infusion.
**And it flips the conclusion:**
- at **3.18x** Shriram is fairly priced against Sundaram (3.37x on 15.0% RoE) and a full turn
  behind Bajaj (5.93x) and Chola (5.24x) — the gap closes only if RoE actually climbs
- at **2.36x** it is statistically **cheap** for a 16.4% RoE — cheaper than Sundaram at a similar
  RoE, with re-rating room still open

**Resolve the live book value per share before sizing anything on the multiple.** This is a good
example of why an aggregator ratio should never be the basis of a decision after a corporate action.

## Fundamentals

**AUM ₹313,798 crore, +15.26% YoY, +3.81% QoQ.** NII ₹8,055.70 crore, +33.67%. FY26 PAT
₹9,998.15 crore.

**Segment mix is not disclosed** — the filing states there are "no separate reportable segments
identified as per Ind AS 108." The CV/PV/MSME/gold/personal split exists only in the investor
presentation, which was unreachable. **A real gap on a lender whose largest book is cyclical.**

**Asset quality reads mid-cycle, not late-cycle:**

| | Jun-25 | Mar-26 | Jun-26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross Stage 3 | 4.53% | 4.58% | **4.64%** |
| Net Stage 3 | 2.57% | 2.33% | 2.33% |
| Provision coverage | 44.31% | 50.34% | **50.99%** |

Gross slippage drifting up while net holds flat and **coverage builds from 44% to 51%** — that is
provisioning running *ahead* of stress rather than chasing it. Not a downturn signal. Watch the
gross trend.

## The governance catch that matters

**"Shri Ram Finance Corporation Pvt" — fined ₹10.80 lakh by the RBI in August 2026 — is a
different, small, private NBFC. It is NOT the listed Shriram Finance Limited.**

That name collision is exactly the sort of thing that produces a false red flag and kills a
position wrongly, or the reverse mistake and misses a real one. **One older item — "RBI Fines HDFC
Bank and Shriram Finance," Outlook Money, 12 July 2025 — was not disambiguated and remains open.**

Nothing else found: no auditor issue, no promoter pledging, no SEBI action. **Treat that as "not
found in this search," not as "confirmed clean"** — depth was limited by the search cap.

## The NBFC backdrop — one item is a genuine tailwind I did not have

**The RBI restored the pre-November-2023 risk weights on bank lending to NBFCs on 25 February
2026**, rolling back the 2023 tightening, confirmed across three outlets. Separately, easier
risk weights on NBFC infrastructure loans took effect **1 April 2026**. Both loosen NBFC funding.

- **ECL provisioning is mandatory from April 2027** — headlines specify banks; **NBFC applicability
  is unconfirmed** and matters a great deal for this sector.
- NBFC retail loans are now **57% of NBFC portfolios**; securitisation hit a record **₹2.55 lakh
  crore in FY26**.
- **Microfinance has turned but from a brutal base — the sector portfolio shrank 18.3% year on
  year.** CreditAccess Grameen posted Q1 FY27 profit **+720%**. The 3-lender rule and ₹2 lakh
  household cap are credited with curbing the bad-loan build.
- Funding: bond yields rose through H1 pressuring margins; NBFCs shifted to floating-rate issuance.
  **No rating-downgrade or liquidity-stress signal** — this is margin compression, not solvency.

## The view

**Accumulate on weakness, do not chase here, and do not size fully before Q2 FY27 (~October).**

The two things that would make this a clean re-rating story are both **unconfirmed**: a filed
decline in cost of funds (only guidance exists), and a settled read on CV asset quality (gross
Stage 3 is still drifting up). Capital adequacy alone does not re-rate a lender — **funding cost
and RoE delivery do.**

**Key risk:** the commercial-vehicle book is the largest and most cyclical exposure. If CV volumes
roll over before the MUFG funding benefit reaches the P&L, this becomes a credit-cost story
instead of a re-rating story — and a stock that has already roughly doubled has little cushion.

**What changes the view:** a Q2/Q3 print showing cost of funds actually down **in the filed
numbers**, and gross Stage 3 stabilising. Then buy through ₹1,100-1,200 rather than waiting.
**Conversely:** gross Stage 3 above ~4.8-5.0%, or no funding-cost improvement by Q3 FY27, and the
re-rating has already happened.

**Corroboration from our own data:** revision breadth is **18 up / 8 down, diffusion +0.38, FY+1
EPS +9.37% over 90 days** ([[revision-breadth-is-now-collected]]) — a genuine upgrade cycle,
consistent with the constructive-but-unproven read.
