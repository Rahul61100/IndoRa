# The August bitcoin bounce was a $3bn short squeeze, not fresh demand

Verified 2026-08-26. **This changes the character of the move I described as "ETF-driven".**

**What actually happened on 19-20 August:** BTC broke $71,000 and **$3bn of shorts were liquidated
in 24 hours against just $263.5mn of longs** — the largest short-liquidation event since at least
2021, with over $1bn cleared in a single hour. On 21 August it broke $75,000, liquidating roughly
another $1bn of shorts.

**A short squeeze is mechanically different from a demand-driven rally.** It clears one specific
type of positioning; it does not repair demand, and it can leave an air pocket once complete. The
+21% month is real price action built on forced covering.

**Corrections to my own timeline:** the actual 2026 cycle low was **~$59,100 in early June**, not
the August low of $62,844. And the drawdown began with a **$19bn liquidation cascade in October
2025 near the $126k top** — the all-time high was immediately followed by the event that started
the decline.

## The on-chain picture is genuinely mixed, not accumulation

**Constructive:**
- Price sits **above the short-term-holder cost basis of ~$67,000** — support-confirming
- Broad 155-day long-term-holder supply hit an all-time high of **16.64M BTC (~83% of supply)**
- **MVRV 1.20, Z-score 0.42** — closer to reset territory than euphoria

**Not constructive:**
- **Exchange balances are RISING**, from ~2.67M BTC in May to ~2.73M in mid-August. That breaks
  the long secular outflow trend and cuts directly against the "supply is leaving exchanges" case.
  Binance alone added ~50,000 BTC over three months.
- The narrower **1-year+ holder cohort distributed 356k BTC (-2.9%)**, dropping below a 60% supply
  share
- 44,300 BTC moved to exchanges on 20 August — the largest short-term-holder profit-taking of 2026

**Derivatives:** funding 0.0049%/8h (~5.3% annualised) — positive but not extreme. Open interest
~$55.9bn, +16.7% in a week. **Options 25-delta risk reversal at -3.61% across tenors — still
put-skewed, meaning hedging demand persists even as spot rallies.**

## The one genuinely bullish data point

**ETF flows.** Five consecutive days of net inflows through 25 August — 19 Aug +$517.2mn, 20 Aug
+$606.3mn, 21 Aug +$307.5mn, 24 Aug +$337.6mn, 25 Aug +$314.3mn — roughly **$2.08bn over five
sessions**, holding through the recovery rather than reversing on strength. That is real external
capital, distinct from the squeeze.

Cumulative since the January 2024 launch: **~$54.4bn**, AUM ~**$95.77bn** across 12 spot BTC ETFs
(IBIT alone $58.78bn), holding roughly **1.225M BTC, about 6.2% of supply**. ETH ETFs: ~$12.45bn
cumulative, ~$14.17bn AUM.

**How to apply:** separate the squeeze from the flow. The flow is the part that can persist; the
squeeze is spent. Given the move was short-covering-led, **the macro trigger for the *next* move
matters more than usual**. Related: [[stablecoin-supply-peaked-in-may]], [[the-yen-carry-trade-has-reloaded]].
