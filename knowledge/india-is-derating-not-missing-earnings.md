# India is de-rating while earnings accelerate — a flows problem, not an earnings problem

As of 2026-08-26. Nifty 50 at 24,207.8, **-2.7% over one year** and 8.1% below its 52-week high,
sitting below its 200 DMA. Sensex -4.7% over the year.

Against that: **Q1 FY27 Nifty 50 profit grew ~18% year on year, the best in ten quarters**, on
revenue up 19.4%, the best in nine. Analysts had modelled 10%. Sector leadership in earnings was
BFSI +20%, metals +53%, technology +11%, autos +7%.

Meanwhile the S&P 500 is +19.3% and the Nasdaq +21.8% over the same year. India has
underperformed US equities by roughly 22 points.

**Why it matters:** the bearish case for India is usually argued as "growth is slowing". The data
says profits are accelerating and the multiple is contracting. Those need opposite responses —
a multiple problem resolves when flows turn, and flows turn on relative return, not on domestic
fundamentals.

**How to apply:** do not use weak index price action as evidence of weak earnings. Watch the
India-vs-US relative return line and FPI flows as the actual driver.
