# The Fed's next move may be up, and the long end is already there

Verified 2026-08-26. Chair is **Kevin Warsh**.

- **Fed funds 3.50-3.75%**, held for a **fifth consecutive meeting** in July 2026
- **Three FOMC members dissented in favour of a 25bp hike**; nine of eighteen dots show a
  preference for at least one hike this year
- Markets have shifted from pricing cuts to pricing the possibility of **hikes**
- **10-year 4.7%**, close to a 20-month high of 4.75%. 5-year 4.4%. **30-year 5.2%.**
- Driver is fiscal: wider deficits plus heavy corporate debt supply. Inflation is above target
  partly on energy, magnified by the US-Iran tanker blockade in the Persian Gulf.

Bond proxies confirm it: TLT -4.2% over a year and in a downtrend, within 3% of its 52-week low;
LQD -3.0% and also in a downtrend. HYG is roughly flat — credit is soft, not broken.

**Why it matters:** long rates at these levels are the discount-rate headwind under every
long-duration asset, and they are the reason a $23.7bn free-cash-flow deficit at Oracle is
suddenly a solvency question rather than a growth story. It also removes the "global easing"
support from every emerging-market bull case, India included.

**How to apply:** reject theses whose engine is falling rates in *any* market. Watch the 30-year
rather than the 10-year — that is where the fiscal premium shows first.
