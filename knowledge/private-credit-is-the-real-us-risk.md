# Investment-grade spreads say nothing is wrong; the low-quality tail says otherwise

Verified 2026-08-26. Credit is bifurcated, and only one half is visible in the headline indices.

**The calm half:** credit spreads are near historical lows globally, including sub-investment
grade, even after borrowing hit a new peak in 2025. Our own proxies agree — HYG is roughly flat
over a year and 1.8% off its high.

**The half that is not calm:**
- **US private credit default rate hit a record 6.0% in April 2026** (Fitch). Private-credit-backed
  corporate borrowers defaulted at **9.2% in 2025**.
- The headline private-credit default rate has printed below 2% for years, but once selective
  defaults and liability-management exercises are counted the **"true" rate approaches 5%**.
- **CCC default rates are materially above long-run averages**, and the spread differential
  between CCC-and-lower and single-B US high yield now **exceeds 600bp**. The junk market is
  pricing stress the equity market has not.
- Jamie Dimon's 2026 shareholder letter warned private-credit losses will be "higher than
  expected" and criticised the industry's lack of rigorous valuation marks.

Feared transmission is not bank failure but **contagion** — leveraged finance, regional banks,
insurers and pension funds pressured simultaneously.

**Why it matters:** this is the same variable underneath [[ai-capex-is-now-a-credit-story]].
Oracle is raising **$40bn** in FY27 with a **-$23.7bn** free cash flow deficit, into a market
where the low-quality tail is already repricing and the 30-year is at 5.2%
([[fed-may-hike-next]]). AI-related debt issuance is expected to more than double to ~$570bn in
2026. Those are the same lenders.

**How to apply:** stop reading IG or HYG as "credit is fine" — the average is hiding the tail.
Track the **CCC-minus-B differential** as the real gauge, and treat KRE (regional banks) as the
equity tell for contagion. Any thesis that requires cheap capital for a cash-burning issuer is
now a credit thesis whether or not it is written as one.
