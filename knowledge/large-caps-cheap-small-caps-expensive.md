# Indian large caps are cheap and small/mid caps are expensive — the reverse of the usual advice

Valuations as of August 2026, from index aggregators (trailing, source-dependent — name the
source when quoting):

- **Nifty 50 P/E ~20.4**, roughly 12.6% *below* its 10-year average
- **Nifty Midcap 100 ~28-29x**, a 26-30% *premium* to its ~22.5x long-term average
- **Nifty Smallcap 250 P/E ~34.4**, ~22% above its 5-year median of 28.3
- Small caps carry roughly a **71% premium to large caps** on trailing earnings

But momentum runs the other way: Nifty Midcap 50 is in an uptrend, +15.4% over a year and within
0.2% of its 52-week high, while the Nifty 50 is in a downtrend and -2.7%.

**Why it matters:** the cheap segment is falling and the expensive segment is rising. Valuation
alone has been the wrong single variable for over a year.

**How to apply:** stay large cap on valuation grounds but do not expect the gap to close on a
schedule, and size accordingly. Related: [[value-and-momentum-are-split]].
