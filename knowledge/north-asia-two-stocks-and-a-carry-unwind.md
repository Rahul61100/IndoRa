# North Asia: the KOSPI is two stocks, and Japan is a carry unwind waiting

Follow-up to [[india-vs-north-asia-the-trade-already-happened]], now with the structural detail that
turns "don't chase it" into a specific, watchable set of conditions.

## The KOSPI is more than half a two-stock bet

**Samsung Electronics + SK Hynix are 55.5% of KOSPI total market capitalisation** as of mid-2026,
**up from 22% at end-2023.** SK Hynix is up 10.1× and Samsung 5.6× since June 2025.

An index that doubled while two constituents went from a fifth to over half of it did not have a
broad rally. It had a two-stock rally with an index wrapped around it. Anyone buying "Korea" as a
country allocation is buying memory semiconductors with a small equity portfolio attached.

**Ex-Samsung and SK Hynix, KOSPI forward P/E was 12× (UBS, May 2026)** — described as reasonable.
That is the real Korea, and it did not participate.

## The valuation paradox, resolved

KOSPI's blended P/E has fallen to **its lowest since the 2008 financial crisis** while the index
roughly doubled. Both facts are true because **Samsung and SK Hynix earnings grew faster than their
share prices.**

That is the arithmetic of a cyclical peak stated as a valuation virtue. The multiple is not low
because the market is cheap; it is low because the denominator is at a level nobody believes
persists. This is the same reading arrived at independently from SK Hynix's own forward P/E of 3.7
against a 76% operating margin.

## The tell

SK Hynix Q2 2026 (quarter to June, reported 28–29 July): revenue **KRW 79.3tn, +257% YoY**;
operating profit **~KRW 60.5tn (~$41.7bn), +557% YoY**, a **76% operating margin**. Verified
independently against the income statement here.

**The stock fell ~10% on that print.** "Record profit misses forecasts, shares slump 10%"
(Reuters, 29 July).

A company that grew operating profit 557% and lost a tenth of its value in a day is a company whose
expectations have outrun even spectacular delivery. That single reaction is worth more than any
multiple: it says the marginal buyer is already fully invested in the thesis. **It is also the
cleanest confirmation available that the cyclical-top read is right.**

The tape agrees it is contested — the >100% YTD gain is net of at least two double-digit
drawdowns (−10% around 23 June, another ~−6% around 20 August), and the index is 24% off its high
with three separate days above +10% in two years.

## Japan is a different risk with the same root

- Nikkei ~66,100–66,400, **+55% over the trailing year**, already **~9% off its 13 August high**,
  including a single-day fall of 2,694 points (>4%) on 17 July on semiconductor weakness.
- **BOJ raised to 1.0% on 16 June 2026 — a 31-year high** — held there on 30–31 July while warning
  core inflation may exceed target. Sell-side paths reach 1.5% by 2027.
- USD/JPY **159.39** (matches the 159.415 derived independently here from intraday prints).
- **Yen short (carry) positioning hit a nine-year high** around the June BOJ decision.
- **Japan and the United States executed a rare JOINT FX intervention to support the yen on 2–3
  August 2026** — coordinated, not unilateral MOF action, with both signalling readiness for more.
  Size is not reconciled across sources (¥5tn / $36.6bn / $46.9bn — same order of magnitude, not
  the same number).
- Foreign ownership of Japanese equities is at a record above one third, with a half-year record
  **$60bn** of foreign buying in H1 2026.

**A hiking central bank, a nine-year-high short base, and a joint intervention are the three
ingredients of a carry unwind.** The 2024 episode is the template: the unwind does not stay in
Japan, because the funding currency finances positions everywhere. Record foreign ownership is the
transmission channel, not a vote of confidence.

## What to watch, rather than what to buy

Neither market is a buy here. Both are watchlist entries with conditions:

- **Korea**: entry is margin *stabilisation* at a lower level with the multiple **re-expanding** —
  the bottom of a memory cycle, not the top. Watch the multiple, not the margin; everyone can see
  the margin.
- **The memory cycle is the India link.** If SK Hynix's margin rolls over from 76%, it is a global
  electronics capex signal that reaches Indian EMS names through the BOM channel already flagged
  against Dixon. That makes this a leading indicator for a market I do hold, which is a better
  reason to track it than the return table.
- **Japan**: the risk is the funding currency, not the index. USD/JPY breaking decisively below the
  intervention zone with BOJ hiking is the unwind signal, and it would hit every risk asset —
  including India, which is not insulated by being uninvolved.

## Gaps left open

- Nikkei/TOPIX forward P/E against its historical average could **not** be retrieved despite
  targeted searching. A genuine gap, not a zero.
- TAIEX at 45,975 (+89.7% YTD) with TWD at a 14-month low in July and an August rebound Bloomberg
  called "fragile" — the USD return for Taiwan is materially different from the local one and was
  not pinned down.
- KOSPI's 52-week high shows as 9,385 on one source against 9,114 on another; the higher figure
  looks anomalous against the news flow and is unresolved.
