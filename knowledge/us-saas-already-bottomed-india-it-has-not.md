# The SaaSpocalypse already reversed in the US; Indian IT is still trading the fear

**The single most valuable thing the multi-market pipeline has produced.** Verified 2026-08-26.

**United States.** In early February 2026 roughly **$1 trillion of enterprise software value
vanished in a week** on fears that AI agents would make SaaS obsolete. By mid-April, disclosures
from the large software names showed AI was *accelerating* revenue pipelines through
non-traditional channels — the market moved from counting seats to counting RPO and compute
workloads. SaaS EV/forward-revenue multiples recovered from **3.9x to 4.7x** on 2026 estimates
during Q2, and SaaS stocks gained **18.9% in the June quarter against the S&P's 14.9%**.

Our own basket confirms it: Software-AI-Exposed is **+19.6% over three months and +12.6% over one
month with 83% of members above their 200 DMA**, even though it is still -7.5% over a year.
Recovery is real but incomplete — the group needs roughly another 40-50% to recover mid-2025
levels.

**India.** Nifty IT is down 40-50% from its peak, worst since 2008, roughly ₹19.28 lakh crore of
value gone. Infosys -24.7% over a year, TCS -25.7%, Wipro -28.7%. The Indian IT basket is
**+5.9% over three months with only 43% breadth**. Infosys *trimmed* FY27 guidance at Q1.

**Why it matters:** the same narrative — AI destroys the software business model — broke the US
group in February and had visibly reversed there by April. India is trading a fear the US has
already partly discarded, roughly four to six months behind.

**Where the read-across breaks, and this matters:** US SaaS is product with software gross
margins; Indian IT is labour arbitrage. If AI compresses billable headcount, Indian IT takes a
*structurally* worse hit than a SaaS vendor that can attach AI to a seat price. The two are not
the same asset. What transfers is the sentiment cycle, not the economics.

**How to apply:** treat the US software basket as a **leading indicator for Indian IT sentiment,
never as proof of Indian IT earnings.** The confirming signal on the India side remains an actual
guidance *raise*, not a US re-rating. Track the two baskets' 3-month returns side by side each
day; the gap closing is the setup. Related: [[nifty-it-derated-on-ai-disruption]],
[[cross-market-lead-lag-is-the-edge]].
