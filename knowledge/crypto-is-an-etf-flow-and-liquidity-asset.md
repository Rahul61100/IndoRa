# Crypto now trades as an ETF-flow and liquidity asset, not an independent one

Verified 2026-08-26. BTC **$78,199.8** — **-9.2% over a year and 19.3% below its high** near
$126,000, but **+21.1% in a month and +28.2% over three** with RSI 78. ETH $2,450.8, -13.4% over
a year but **+51.3% over three months**, RSI 76.

The rally is explicitly flow-driven, not narrative-driven:
- US spot bitcoin ETFs took **$517mn on 19 Aug**, the largest daily inflow since early May, and
  roughly **$1.6bn Monday to Thursday** that week. IBIT alone took ~$239mn on 21 Aug.
- Treasury Secretary Bessent signalling further intervention loosened financial conditions and
  pressured the dollar. ETF investors returned after months of withdrawals.

Structure of the move, from the baskets: **Majors +28.8% over three months with 100% breadth**,
DeFi +36.5% with 75%, Oracles (LINK) +52.5%. But **L1-Alt is -38.5% over a year with only 22%
breadth** and **L2 is -54.8% with 0%**. This is a **majors-and-ETF rally, not an alt season.**
73% of tracked crypto is above its 50 DMA but only 56% above its 200 DMA — a young recovery
inside an unrepaired downtrend.

Targets on offer: $80-95k conservative, above $100k optimistic, Bernstein $150k end-2026 with a
$200k cycle peak in 2027.

**Why it matters:** if the driver is ETF flows and dollar liquidity, then crypto is a
**high-beta expression of the same liquidity variable that drives equities** — not a diversifier
and not a hedge. It should be modelled against the dollar, real yields and ETF creations, not
against blockchain adoption.

**How to apply:** track IBIT/ETHA flows and DXY as the primary inputs. Treat RSI 78 on BTC and
79-81 on the ETF vehicles as a stretched short-term condition inside a recovery, not as a
breakout. **No position taken.** Related: [[crypto-regulation-timeline-2026]].
