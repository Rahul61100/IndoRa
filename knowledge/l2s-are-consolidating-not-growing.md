# Ethereum's L2s are consolidating to three winners; the rest are being wound down

Verified 2026-08-26. Explains the starkest reading in the crypto snapshot: the **L2 basket is
-54.8% over a year with 0% of members above their 200 DMA**, and ARB printed an RSI of 0.

**The cause is economic, and it was self-inflicted.** The Dencun upgrade cut fees ~90%, which
triggered a fee war that pushed most rollups into losses. **Base was the only L2 that turned a
profit in 2025, at roughly $55mn.**

**Concentration is now extreme:**
- Base + Arbitrum hold **over 77% of L2 DeFi TVL** (Base 46.58%, Arbitrum 30.86%); adding
  Optimism's ~6% takes the top three to **83%**
- The big three process close to **90% of all L2 transactions**, Base alone over 60%
- Usage at the smaller rollups is **down 61%**

**The wind-downs are already happening:** Linea's deposits fell from $976mn in November 2025 to
$367mn by May 2026 (-60%); **Blast's TVL collapsed 97%**; Kinto shut down entirely; Loopring
closed its wallet service. 21Shares' view is that most of the remaining 50-plus rollups do not
survive 2026.

**The lesson underneath it:** points-and-incentive-driven TVL was rented attention. It evaporated
the moment rewards stopped, revealing that most general-purpose rollups had no differentiated
demand at all.

**How to apply:** treat "L2" as a **dead sector with three survivors**, not a growth category, and
do not read a beaten-down L2 token as value. This is also the cleanest available case study in
infrastructure with no value accrual — the technology worked, throughput went up, fees went to
zero, and no equity holder captured anything. Keep it as the reference example when any
infrastructure thesis in any market claims that adoption implies returns.
