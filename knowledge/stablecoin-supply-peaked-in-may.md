# Stablecoin supply peaked in May 2026 and is shrinking while BTC rallies

**Derived from our own collected series, not from reporting** — `data/flows/stablecoin_supply.json`,
DefiLlama, first pulled 2026-08-26.

| Date | Total stablecoin supply |
|---|---|
| 2025-08-26 | $277.9bn |
| **2026-05-17 (peak)** | **$320.9bn** |
| 2026-05-31 | $317.6bn |
| 2026-08-25 | $308.8bn |

**+11.2% over a year, but -3.2% over 90 days and flat (+0.3%) over 30.**

This cross-validates the external reporting ($320bn all-time high in May, ~$308bn in August) —
good, the collector is trustworthy. But it also says something the reporting framed as pure
growth: **the on-chain dollar base has been contracting for three months.**

Set that against the same window's price action: **BTC +28.2% and ETH +51.3% over three months**,
driven by spot-ETF creations ([[crypto-is-an-etf-flow-and-liquidity-asset]]).

**The divergence is the finding.** Two different pools of money are behaving in opposite ways:
- **ETF flows** — TradFi money buying exposure through a wrapper, rising
- **Stablecoin supply** — capital actually resident on-chain and available to deploy, falling

The rally is being funded from outside the crypto system, not from inside it. Corroborated by
**DeFi TVL at $87.6bn, -41.5% over a year** even as the DeFi token basket is +36.5% over three
months — prices are recovering well ahead of the capital that would justify them, and by the L2
economics collapsing outright ([[l2s-are-consolidating-not-growing]]).

**How to apply:** treat stablecoin supply as the measure of **native** crypto liquidity and ETF
creations as the measure of **imported** liquidity. A rally on imported liquidity alone is
reversible at the speed of the flow that created it, and it does not broaden — which is exactly
what the baskets show (Majors at 100% breadth, L1-Alt at 22%, L2 at 0%). Watch for stablecoin
supply turning up as the confirmation that the move has become self-sustaining. It has not yet.
