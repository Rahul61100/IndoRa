# Who is actually buying India — and the HDFC Bank de-rating was one quarter of FII selling

Verified 2026-08-26. **Confidence: DEGRADED** — ran after the session search cap, via Google News
RSS and direct fetches to NSE/AMFI/Trendlyne after most publishers blocked WebFetch. Individual
figures need primary re-verification; the pattern is consistent across many independent items.

## The single most useful fact found

**HDFC Bank: FIIs cut their stake 360bps in the March 2026 quarter — roughly ₹35,000 crore sold.**
The largest stock-level FII sale value found in the entire sweep.

That is a **mechanical flow explanation for a −26% de-rating**, not a fundamental one. It sits
alongside the NIM story ([[hdfc-bank-nim-is-structural]]) rather than replacing it — but a forced
seller of that size eventually finishes, and a NIM trough does not.

**And the reversal may already be starting.** As of **22 August 2026**: *"Financials stage a FII
comeback, but the ₹1.06 lakh crore gap remains."* Banks were sold at roughly ₹1,100 crore a day
through H1; that has partially turned.

## The IT exodus was also flows, not just narrative

- **₹17,000 crore of FII selling in Indian IT in February 2026 alone**; ₹10,956 crore in the first
  half of that month, taking **FII IT holdings to a four-year low**
- **"FIIs exit 112 stocks in three years: a $9bn IT exodus"**

So the Nifty IT collapse ([[nifty-it-derated-on-ai-disruption]]) is an AI narrative **plus** a
sustained, quantified foreign withdrawal. Positioning is now emptied out — which is a contrarian
setup, but only once revisions stop falling.

## Promoters turned buyers in March, after two years of selling

- **"Promoters turn selective buyers in the March quarter after two years of heavy selling"**
- **"$4 billion in 2026: India Inc promoters pump into own stocks amid the selloff"**
- **Reliance promoter group: ~₹8,500-9,000 crore of creeping acquisition, stake to 50.48%,
  17 July 2026.** The largest single insider buy found.
- Also buying into their own weakness: Burman family into Religare (to ~30%), Godrej Properties
  promoters (5% for ₹2,674 crore), Kumar Mangalam Birla into Vodafone Idea, Adani Energy
  promoters (to 74.29%), Sula (first purchase since its 2022 IPO).

Insider buying picked up **precisely when the narrative was most cautious.**

## Pledge releases dominate, which is the healthy direction

IndusInd (₹7,667 crore released), **Vedanta** (encumbrance released on 50.1% of pledged stock, and
restrictions lifted 25 Aug after debt repayment), JSW Steel (16m shares), **Navin Fluorine
(final release — pledge to zero)**, Lloyds Metals, Anupam Rasayan, Wanbury (pledge to zero).

Increases — the warning list — are small and specific: Jyoti CNC, Cineline, Bharat Wire Ropes, and
**Kalyan Jewellers (MF selling *plus* rising promoter pledge together)**.

## The supply nobody counts: PE exits and government OFS

**Block deals are approaching ₹4 lakh crore in 2026.**

- **PE and VC are exiting listed India at the fastest clip in years** — Lightspeed (PhysicsWallah),
  General Atlantic (Rubicon), TPG (Aster, Sai Life), Bain (Emcure), Peak XV (MobiKwik), Elevation
  (Paytm), Madison and Actis (Pine Labs), True North (Fedbank), Brighton Park (Indegene),
  Mylan/Viatris (Biocon ₹3,481 crore). **~$32bn of listed PE holdings still await monetisation.**
- **Government disinvestment is the dominant OFS source** — LIC 6.5% for **₹31,552 crore**, Coal
  India 2% for ₹5,549 crore, IRFC twice, BHEL, Central Bank, GIC Re, NLC, Cochin Shipyard.

**Add that to the ₹4.72 trillion IPO pipeline and the NSE issue**
([[nse-ipo-is-the-supply-event-that-matters]]) and the supply side is far larger than I had it.

**But note who is on the other side: Indian mutual funds are absorbing almost every block** —
SBI MF, ICICI Pru, Nippon, Kotak, Edelweiss, Bandhan each buying directly from a PE seller. SBI MF
took ₹5,748 crore of Adani stock from GQG in one go.

## Two DII facts that cut against the consensus reading

1. **DIIs bought 82% of Nifty 50 names in July while FIIs sold 72% of them.** The "foreigners are
   fleeing" narrative and the "domestic money is aggressively buying" reality are the same market
   at the same time.
2. **Large-cap fund inflows turned NEGATIVE in July 2026 — the first time since December 2023** —
   while SIP totals hit ₹31,961 crore and **small-cap inflows rose 39%.** Domestic retail is
   quietly rotating **down** the cap curve into the segment trading at 38x and two sigma rich
   ([[india-smid-premium-is-two-sigma]]), exactly as headlines call SIP flows stable and
   unstoppable. **That is where the risk is building.**

**MF sector positioning:** overweight healthcare, capital goods (a 17-month high) and **PSU banks
(a three-year high)**; underweight IT.

## How to apply

multi-year low by a forced seller, the de-rating has a natural end date that fundamentals do not.

`block.csv` are confirmed free and machine-readable.
