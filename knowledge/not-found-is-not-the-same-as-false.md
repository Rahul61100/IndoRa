# I buried a true claim, and the failure was my search, not the source

On 2026-08-27 this workspace demoted **"the US is India's largest remittance source at 27.7%"** to
`UNVERIFIABLE` after five targeted searches failed to retrieve it. The note said: *"do not publish
27.7% without opening the RBI survey table."*

The figure is **real and verified**. It sits in the RBI Bulletin verbatim, in the article *"Changing
Dynamics of India's Remittances — Sixth Round"*, with a clean multi-round series a fabricator would
be unlikely to invent correctly:

**22.9% (2016-17) → 23.4% → 27.7% (FY2023-24)**

The searches failed because the number lives inside one specifically-titled Bulletin article, not
under any generic phrasing of "US largest remittance source India".

## Why this is worth a permanent note

This base has been badly burned three times by confident statistics with no retrievable source — an
ERP R², an 18% tariff, a Reliance EPS cut. Those burns produced a good instinct: demand the primary
source.

They also produced a bad one. **I began treating "I could not find it" as evidence the claim was
false**, and buried a true number on that basis. That is a false negative, and it is the mirror
image of the failure I was guarding against.

Both errors are failures of the same kind: **substituting the ease of retrieval for the truth of
the claim.**

## The rule

**`not_found` is a statement about the search, never about the world.**

Three tiers, kept distinct:

| tier | means |
|---|---|
| `verified` | opened the primary source, saw the number |
| `not_found` | searched and failed. **The claim's truth is unknown.** Record what was tried. |
| `refuted` | found the primary source and it says something else |

The Reliance "FY+1 EPS cut 5.23% in 30 days" was re-searched the same day across sell-side,
data-vendor and press sources and **remains not found** — and real Reliance revisions do exist
(brokers cut FY25-27 EPS by 3.3–9.5% in October 2024), none matching. That one stays buried, but
it stays buried as `not_found`, not as `false`.

**Practical consequence:** before burying a claim, try to find the *document* rather than the
*number*. The 27.7% was unfindable by its value and trivially findable by its container.
