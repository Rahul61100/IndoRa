# US leadership has rotated out of megacap AI into healthcare, financials and small caps

Verified 2026-08-26. This is a broadening, not a top — breadth is *improving* while the leaders
of the last two years stall.

Index level: S&P 7,673.9 (+19.2% 1y), Nasdaq 26,082 (+21.6%), Dow 53,502 (+18.1%), and
**Russell 2000 3,011.2, +28.7% — leading everything.** VIX 15.5, 50% below its 52-week high.
Breadth: **64% of tracked names above their 200 DMA**, 36 uptrend / 17 downtrend / 48 choppy.

**Rotating in:**
- **Healthcare +42.0% over a year with 100% breadth** and +14.9% over three months — the
  strongest *broad* group. XBI +88.2%, in an uptrend, 1.4% off its high. Drivers: MFN drug-pricing
  risk proving narrower than feared, a patent-cliff-driven M&A wave (**$106bn across 201
  transactions in 2026**), and clinical wins. XBI's equal weight is why it beats IBB here.
- **Energy +34.5%, 100% breadth**
- **Financials +17.2% over three months, median RSI 59.7**
- Factor confirmation: **VLUE and QUAL are both within 2% of 52-week highs**, alongside XLF, XLE,
  XLV, XLB, XLRE.
- **Small caps:** ~21% YTD against ~10% for the S&P. Cause is concrete — the OBBBA tax provisions
  took effect 1 Jan 2026, and small-cap earnings are expected to grow **17.1% in 2026 and 18% in
  2027**. Record close 2,888.62 on 6 May.

**Rotating out:**
- **Megacap +15.5% over a year but -7.0% over three months**, 62% breadth
- AI-Datacenter-Power, speculative Space/Quantum, HighBeta-Retail (-33.4% 1y)
- Utilities -3.1% and Staples -4.5%, both with 0-20% breadth

**Why it matters:** the concentration risk everyone worried about is resolving upward — the index
is holding while leadership widens. That is the healthy version.

**How to apply:** contrast with India, where breadth is 56% and split three ways
([[breadth-is-split-not-trending]]). The US is a market where an index view is worth having and
India is not. Related: [[ai-capex-is-now-a-credit-story]], [[cross-market-lead-lag-is-the-edge]].
