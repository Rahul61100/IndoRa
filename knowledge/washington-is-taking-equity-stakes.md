# Washington is taking equity stakes in companies — this is state capitalism, and it is a factor

Verified 2026-08-26. **Confidence: DEGRADED** — several deal terms rest on secondary aggregation
because agency sites blocked automated access.

The US government is now a direct shareholder in a series of listed companies, which changes how
those names should be underwritten.

| Company | Mechanism | Terms | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intel (INTC)** | equity + warrant | **9.9%**, 433.3m shares at $20.47 ≈ **$8.9bn**; passive, no board seat; +5% warrant if foundry ownership falls below 51% | 22 Aug 2025 |
| **MP Materials (MP)** | preferred equity | $400m — **DoD becomes largest shareholder**, with a **$110/kg NdPr price floor** | 10 Jul 2025 |
| **Lithium Americas (LAC)** | two 5% stakes | company + Thacker Pass JV with GM, via a restructured $2.26bn DOE loan | ~Oct 2025 |
| **USA Rare Earth (USAR)** | equity + debt | 10% stake; $1.6bn package including $1.3bn senior secured | ~Jan 2026 |
| **IBM / GlobalFoundries** | equity | $1bn / $375m, part of a ~$2.0bn nine-company quantum programme | ~May 2026 |
| **Trilogy Metals (TMQ)** | equity LOI | 10%, ~$35.6m — **still not closed as of 3 Aug 2026** | LOI Oct 2025 |
| **US Steel** | **golden share** | presidential approval required to move HQ, redomicile, rename, cut the $14bn investment pledge, offshore production or idle plants; president appoints 1 of 3 board seats | closed 18 Jun 2025 |

Plus large directed credit: **Southern Company $26-26.5bn — the largest DOE loan in history** ·
Westinghouse $17.5bn for up to 10 AP1000 reactors · Perpetua $2.9bn EXIM · AEP $1.6bn and $3.2bn.

**The MP Materials structure is the one to study — a government price floor turns a commodity
producer into something closer to a regulated utility.** That is a different asset class, not a
better commodity stock.

**Two other mechanisms worth knowing:**
- **Nvidia and AMD agreed to pay the US government 15% of China AI-chip revenue** as a condition
  of export licences (Aug 2025)
- **Section 48D semiconductor investment credit was raised from 25% to 35%** for property placed
  in service after 31 Dec 2025 — a direct, legislated boost to TSMC, Micron, Intel, GlobalFoundries

**A governance flag, stated as what it is:** Vulcan Elements — backed by 1789 Capital, which is
linked to Donald Trump Jr. — received a **$620m conditional DoD loan** (the largest ever from the
Office of Strategic Capital) plus a $50m Commerce/Pentagon pledge. **Democratic lawmakers have
requested a Pentagon Inspector General probe into $3.2bn cumulative in contracts and loans to
Trump-family-backed firms. That is a request for an investigation, not a finding.**

Separately and confirmed at primary-source tier: **Trump's OGE disclosure shows $1.4 billion in
crypto income in his first year back in office**, largely from meme coins, and the **OCC granted
World Liberty Financial a conditional national trust-bank charter on 14 August 2026** for its USD1
stablecoin. Ownership figures for WLFI do not reconcile across dates and should be presented as
separately dated claims, never as a cap table.

**And a structural execution risk:** the **SEC is running 2 of 5 commissioners, the CFTC roughly 1
of 5, the FTC 2 of 5, and DOJ Antitrust is vacant** — all while attempting the largest crypto
rulemaking in US history and continuing major antitrust litigation. **GENIUS already missed its own
one-year rulemaking deadline in July 2026.**

**How to apply:** where the state holds equity, a price floor, or a golden share, the downside is
policy-supported and the upside is politically constrained. Underwrite those names on **policy
durability**, not on the commodity or the cycle. And treat regulatory *capacity* as a real
variable — understaffed agencies miss deadlines, which is itself tradeable.
