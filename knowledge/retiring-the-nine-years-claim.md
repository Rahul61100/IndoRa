# "Nine consecutive years of consensus misses" is retired

This base has repeated, as settled fact, that **Indian consensus earnings estimates have missed for
nine consecutive years.** It appeared in the load-bearing list, in position notes, and in the
framing of the Q2 FY27 preview.

A dedicated search ran **15+ differently-phrased queries** — the exact phrase, "ten years in a row",
"Groundhog Day", and by name the analysts most likely to have said it (Kotak's Prasad, Motilal
Oswal, Marcellus, Jefferies' Chris Wood). **Zero sources state any specific consecutive-year count.**

## Applying this base's own rule to itself

[[not-found-is-not-the-same-as-false]] says `not_found` is a statement about the search, never about
the world — written after I wrongly buried a *true* claim. So this is **not** a declaration that the
nine-year pattern is false.

It is a decision about what may be *asserted*. Fifteen targeted searches including named-analyst
queries is real effort, and a number this specific with no locatable source is the exact shape of
the three claims that already burned this workspace: an ERP R², an 18% tariff, a Reliance EPS cut.

**Retired means: stop repeating it.** Not disproved, not deleted — no longer usable as support for
anything.

## What replaces it, narrower and dated

- A genuine FY27 downgrade cycle: consensus EPS cut **~9% cumulatively**, hitting **62–66% of Nifty
  constituents** through spring 2026. [reported, cross-confirmed]
- And that cycle has now **turned**: Q1 FY27 Nifty PAT **+18% YoY, a 10-quarter high.**

## A collision worth flagging rather than smoothing

That +18% needs care, because **this base already corrected an "+18%" earnings claim** — recorded as
"one quarter with 60% of the growth from five stocks", used to overturn a bullish read.

Two possibilities, and I cannot yet separate them:

1. These are **different quarters** and Q1 FY27 is a genuine new datapoint.
2. They are **the same quarter**, and it is now being cited back to me as evidence *for* the bullish
   case it was originally used to demolish.

Until that is resolved, **the +18% may not be used in either direction.** A number that has already
been through one correction cycle in this base does not get a second life without its provenance
re-established.

downgrade-cycle and Q1 figures are **reported** and cross-confirmed two ways (Fortune India, MOFSL).
