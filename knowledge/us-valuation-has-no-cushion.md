Verified 2026-08-26.

**On forward earnings the US premium is +0.3pp; on a cyclically-adjusted basis it is −2.3pp.**
For comparison India's is **−1.9 to −2.1pp** ([[CORRECTION-india-erp-is-negative]]).

So the US is better than India on the forward measure and about the same on the cyclical one.
**Neither market is paying investors to take equity risk.** The US is underwritten by the earnings
trajectory continuing, not by any margin of safety.

Retail is bearish and shorts are elevated. Fund managers at a 28-year cash low have **no dry
powder left to buy a dip**, and cash-level mean reversion has historically preceded drawdowns.

## Concentration — and the genuine offset

Top-10 stocks are **~37.1-37.2% of S&P market cap, against a 2000 dot-com peak of ~27%.** Worse
than the bubble on that measure.

**But equal-weight is beating cap-weight: RSP +15.91% YTD against SPY +12.24%, a 3.7pp gap.**
Unlike 1999-2000, the 2026 rally is **not** solely a mega-cap phenomenon. That is a real structural
difference and it corroborates the breadth finding that says this is not yet a distribution top
([[regime-analog-is-a-splice-not-one-year]]).

**How to apply:** track BofA FMS cash monthly as the crowding gauge and the RSP/SPY spread as the
breadth gauge. **The bear signal to watch is those two diverging** — cash still low while equal
weight starts losing to cap weight would mean crowding without breadth, which is the 2000 and 2007
signature.
