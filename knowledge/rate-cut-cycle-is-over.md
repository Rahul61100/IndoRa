# India's rate-cut cycle is over — nothing may be bought on a "cuts are coming" thesis

As of the August 2026 RBI policy: **repo held at 5.25%**, the fourth consecutive hold since
February 2026, neutral stance retained.

- Repo has already fallen from 6.5% to 5.25% — the easing has been delivered, not promised
- CPI reached **4.4% in June 2026**, above the 4% target for the first time after sixteen
  straight months below it
- RBI projects CPI **peaking at 5.9% in Q3 FY27** (Oct-Dec), FY27 average 5.0%
- RBI raised its FY27 GDP forecast to **6.7%** (against FY26 actual 7.7% on the revised series)

**Why it matters:** a large share of published Indian bull cases still rest on further easing.
The next move is more likely up than down, and the marginal monetary tailwind is gone.

**How to apply:** reject any thesis whose main driver is rate cuts. Bank NIM theses must rest on
liability repricing and mix, which is mechanical, rather than on policy. Rate-sensitive sectors
(realty, autos, NBFC) have already had the benefit. Related: [[hdfc-bank-nim-is-structural]].
