# Demergers make long-window returns meaningless, and Yahoo does not adjust them

Verified 2026-08-26. `TMPV.NS` (Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles) shows roughly **-54% over one
year** in the snapshot. That is the Tata Motors demerger, not a drawdown. `TATAMOTORS.NS` now
returns "no data found" entirely.

Yahoo adjusts splits and bonuses reasonably well; it does **not** reliably adjust Indian
demergers, where value left the entity into a separately listed one.

**Why it matters:** -54% in a year screams "deep value" or "broken business" and is neither.

**How to apply:** before treating any large negative long-window return as signal, check for a
corporate action. Same discipline applies to any ticker that stops resolving — check for a
rename or demerger before concluding delisting. HDFC Bank's 2025 1:1 bonus is the other live
example: its ~₹728 price is not comparable to pre-bonus quotes.
