# US earnings estimates are being revised UP sharply while India's are cut — the cleanest contrast

Verified 2026-08-26 (FactSet). **This is the single clearest reason capital sits where it sits**,
and it is a comparison I could not make before because I was not collecting revisions at all.

**S&P 500 bottom-up EPS, and the revision history:**

| Snapshot | CY2026E | CY2027E |
|---|---|---|
| 13 Feb 2026 | $312.19 | $361.08 |
| 1 May 2026 | $331.23 | $378.64 |
| **7 Aug 2026** | **$358.66** | **$405.17** |
| **3-month revision** | **+8.3%** | **+7.0%** |
| **6-month revision** | **+14.9%** | **+12.2%** |

CY2025 actual was $274.74, so 2026 implies **+30.6%** growth and 2027 **+13.0%**. That is a sharp
and sustained *upward* revision cycle, unusual in magnitude.

**India over the same period: FY27 consensus EPS was CUT roughly 9%**
([[CORRECTION-india-earnings-growth-was-4-5-percent]]), with 66% of Nifty constituents downgraded
in April alone, and the cycle only turning up four weeks ago by +0.6%.

**Estimates rising 15% in one market and falling 9% in another is a bigger explanation of relative
flows than any valuation gap.** It is also why India's MSCI EM weight halved
([[india-lost-half-its-em-weight]]).

## But the US headline is inflated the same way India's was

**Q2 2026 blended net margin printed 16.9% — a record since FactSet began tracking in 2009** (from
14.8% in Q1 and 12.9% a year earlier, against a five-year average of 12.4%).

**It is substantially inflated by two one-time GAAP marks**: Alphabet's ~$98bn unrealised equity
gain and Amazon's ~$53.4bn Anthropic-investment mark. Excluding both, the margin is 15.0% and
**Q2 blended earnings growth falls from 50.4% to 32.0%.**

Still a record. Much smaller record.

**Note the symmetry with my own India error.** I quoted a headline growth figure without checking
its composition and 60% of it turned out to be five stocks. The identical trap is in the US print,
and **the valuation is being set against the headline number, not the clean one.**

**How to apply:** collect revision direction for both markets and put them side by side — it has
done more explanatory work than any multiple. And decompose every headline growth number before
using it. Related: [[us-valuation-has-no-cushion]].
