# India's small-cap premium is more than two standard deviations wide, and the misses are rising

Verified 2026-08-26. Sharper numbers than the earlier
[[large-caps-cheap-small-caps-expensive]] note, which this supersedes on the valuation detail.

- **Nifty Smallcap 250 P/E ~38.28**; Nifty Midcap 150 ~27.18
- **MSCI World Small Cap trades at ~19.5** — Indian small caps are at roughly double the global
  small-cap multiple
- Small caps price a **~71% premium over Indian large caps** on trailing earnings
- The **small-to-large forward P/E ratio is ~1.25x against a long-run average of 0.9x** — more
  than **two standard deviations** above normal and close to prior peaks

And the earnings underneath are getting weaker, not stronger:
- **40% of small caps missed analyst estimates in Q3 FY26, against 25% of large caps**

**Why it matters:** a premium this wide with a rising miss rate is the classic setup for
multiple contraction. It does not date the correction — two-sigma can become three — but it means
the segment carries no valuation cushion at all, and the drawdown when it comes is historically
severe (2008, 2020).

**How to apply:** this workspace stays large cap in India by default. If a small or mid cap
enters the book it needs an idiosyncratic reason that survives a 30% multiple contraction, not a
sector or momentum reason. Note the tension with the tape: Nifty Midcap 50 is in an uptrend within
0.2% of its 52-week high while the Nifty 50 is in a downtrend — **being right on this has been
losing money for over a year** ([[value-and-momentum-are-split]]). Position for it, do not fight
it.
