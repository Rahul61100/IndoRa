## What changed

**The knowledge base and the data pipeline now exist.** Yesterday's analysis was macro and
valuation only. Today added price history, derived technicals, breadth, constituent-based sector
rotation and an automated quality gate. Three of the nine open theses did not survive contact
with the data.

**The quality gate paid for itself on day one.** Every Yahoo `^CNX*` sector index carries a
40-day history hole, rendering the first print after the gap as a fake single-day move — Auto
appeared +7.0%, Metal +8.9%, Media +5.9%. Independent reporting put Nifty Auto's actual 25 Aug
session at +0.4%. Without the flag this would have produced a full day of wrong sector analysis.
Sector rotation now comes from constituent baskets. Rule written to
`playbooks/data-quality-rules.md`.

**The market's real shape is a split, not a trend.** 56% of tracked names above their 200 DMA,
49% above their 50 DMA, and a near-perfect three-way trend split of 24 up / 23 down / 25 choppy.
Index targets are close to worthless at this reading. The money is in sector and stock selection.

**Value and momentum have separated, and the separation is systematic.** Every cheap large cap
in the book is in a confirmed downtrend — HDFC Bank -26%, Infosys -24.7%, TCS -25.7%, ITC -32.1%,
ONGC, Reliance, NTPC all below their 200 DMAs. Every uptrend is in something already re-rated —
BHEL +90.1%, Shriram Finance +81.5%, ABB +50.3%, Divi's +49.7%. Screening on valuation alone
currently builds a portfolio of falling knives.

**Sector truth, from constituents:** capital goods is the broadest uptrend in the market at
+30.2% over a year with **100% of members above their 200 DMA**. EMS is the sharpest turn:
-12.8% over a year but +27.9% over three months, also at 100% breadth. At the other end, oil and
gas has **0% of members above their 200 DMA** — the worst reading in the universe — and FMCG is
at 25% with a median RSI of 38.

**Macro correction to yesterday.** Brent is 26.9% *below* its 52-week high and -8.3% over three
months, with the EIA modelling $69 for 2027. Gold at $4,679 looks like a breakout at +38.7% over
a year, but it is 12% below the $5,589 all-time high set on 28 January and is rebounding inside a
correction that took it down over 20% in its worst quarter since 2013. Silver is the same shape:
+76.6% over a year, 40.7% below its high. India VIX at 10.6 against a 52-week range of 8.72-28.90
— equity weakness has produced no volatility repricing at all.
